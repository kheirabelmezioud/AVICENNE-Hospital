How to Get an Account

If you're a new doctor, you'll need to register with our hospital first to access our online services.

  1. Visit our hospital administration office with valid credentials
  2. Complete the registration form with your professional details
  3. Our staff will verify your information and create your account
  4. You'll receive your account details via email or SMS

For assistance with registration, please call our admin services at 01 40 94 85 85
