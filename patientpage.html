How to Get an Account

If you're a new patient, you'll need to register with our hospital first to access our online services.

  1. Visit our hospital in person with a valid ID
  2. Complete the registration form at the front desk
  3. Our staff will create your account and provide login credentials
  4. You'll receive your account details via email or SMS

For assistance with registration, please call our patient services at 01 40 94 85 85
